# Training & Discipline

## Cadets

Cadets must complete:

* Ride-alongs
* Radio training
* Traffic stop training
* Report writing
* Scenario evaluations

Cadets may **not**:

* Patrol solo
* Join SWAT or tactical units
* Lead a scene

## Officer discipline

| Action | For |
|---|---|
| **Verbal warning** | Minor SOP violations |
| **Written warning** | Repeated misconduct |
| **Suspension** | Major violations or abuse of powers |
| **Termination** | Severe misconduct or corruption |

## Corruption and Internal Affairs

Officers found abusing powers, breaking SOP, metagaming, committing RDM, or creating toxic roleplay may face:

* Suspension
* Demotion
* Termination
* Blacklisting

!!! note "Final word"
    The LSPD exists to improve roleplay for everyone on the server. Officers are expected to create scenarios that are enjoyable, realistic and fair for law enforcement and civilians alike.

    *Protect and Serve.*
