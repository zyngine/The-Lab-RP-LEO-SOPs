# General Conduct

## You must

* Stay in character while on duty
* Treat civilians with respect unless the roleplay genuinely dictates otherwise
* Follow the chain of command
* Use realistic police procedure
* Record major incidents where you can
* Wear approved uniform and use approved vehicles

## You must not

* Abuse your powers or permissions
* Escalate a situation without reason
* Commit FailRP, powergaming or metagaming
* Use more force than the situation calls for
* Break server rules or community guidelines

!!! warning "The badge is not a shield"
    Every rule in the city rulebook applies to you exactly as it applies to a civilian. Being on duty is not a defence for RDM, for metagaming, or for ruining somebody's night.
