# Chain of Command

**Use the chain of command at all times. Do not skip it.**

If your supervisor is unavailable, go to the next rank above them, not straight to the top.

## Board of Commissioners

* Commissioner

## High Command

* Chief of Police
* Assistant Chief
* Deputy Chief

## Command

* Captain
* Lieutenant

## Supervisors

* Master Sergeant
* Sergeant

## Officers

* Senior Corporal
* Corporal
* Senior Officer
* Police Officer
* Cadet

!!! note "Why it matters"
    Skipping the chain is not a shortcut, it is a scene with the wrong people in it. The supervisor you went around is the one who has to answer for the call.
