# Pursuits & PIT

This is the policy you will be held to most precisely. Read it properly.

## When a pursuit may begin

* A suspect flees lawful detention
* The suspect has committed a violent felony
* The risk to the public is justified

## Running the pursuit

* The **primary unit leads**
* The **secondary unit handles radio**
* **Air support takes priority** when available
* Continuously reassess the risk to the public, to officers, and to the suspect
* A supervisor may assume command at any time

## PIT Maneuver

!!! danger "Every one of these must be true before you PIT"
    * The pursuit has been running for at least **3 minutes**
    * A **Sergeant or above** has approved it
    * **One PIT per approval.** Another attempt requires another approval

### Speed restrictions

| Suspect speed | Who may PIT |
|---|---|
| **100 MPH or below** | Any officer, with supervisor approval |
| **Above 100 MPH** | **HEAT Unit personnel only**, still with supervisor approval |

If you are not HEAT, you do not PIT above 100 MPH under any circumstances.

### Before you commit, consider

Traffic density · pedestrians · road conditions · weather · the severity of the crime · the danger to the public

A supervisor may deny or revoke PIT authorisation at any time if the risk outweighs the benefit.

## Terminating a pursuit

A supervisor may terminate a pursuit if:

* The danger to the public becomes excessive
* Visual contact is lost
* The quality of the roleplay is dropping
* It no longer serves any reasonable law enforcement purpose

!!! warning
    **Failure to follow pursuit or PIT policy may result in disciplinary action.** These are the rules most likely to be quoted back at you, by a supervisor or by the driver.
