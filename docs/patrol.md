# Patrol

## Before you go 10-8

* Equip approved gear
* Check your radio
* Fuel and repair your vehicle
* Tell dispatch you are on patrol

## Traffic stops

1. Call out your location and the plate
2. Approach carefully
3. Identify yourself by name and badge number
4. State the reason for the stop
5. Request identification
6. Warning, citation, or arrest as the situation warrants

## Felony stops

* **Two units minimum**, recommended
* Weapons drawn only when appropriate
* Maintain cover and keep talking on the radio
* Follow the escalation procedure

!!! tip "The reason for the stop"
    Saying it out loud is not a formality. It is the thing that gives the driver something to roleplay against, and it is the difference between a scene and a paperwork exercise.
