# Use of Force

## The continuum

Work up it, not straight to the top.

| | Level |
|---|---|
| 1 | Officer presence |
| 2 | Verbal commands |
| 3 | Soft control |
| 4 | Less lethal |
| 5 | Deadly force |

## Deadly force

Authorised **only** when:

* An officer or civilian faces an imminent threat of death or serious bodily harm
* A suspect is actively using a deadly weapon

**Attempt de-escalation whenever it is possible to do so.**

!!! danger "Where officers get this wrong"
    The common failure is skipping straight from verbal commands to a firearm because the suspect was uncooperative. Uncooperative is not a threat to life. Reaching level 5 has to be something the situation did, not something you decided.
