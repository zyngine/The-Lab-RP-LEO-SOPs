# LSPD Standard Operating Procedure

## Mission

The Los Santos Police Department protects and serves the citizens of Los Santos through professional roleplay, fair enforcement of the law, and realistic emergency response, while keeping this a community people want to be part of.

## Core values

**Professionalism** · **Integrity** · **Respect** · **Accountability** · **Teamwork** · **Realistic roleplay**

## How to read this

Everything in the [city rulebook](https://zyngine.github.io/The-Lab-RP-Rulebook/) still applies to you. The badge adds obligations, it does not remove any.

Every procedure here exists because it produces a better scene for the person on the other side of it, not only for us. An arrest nobody had to shoot anyone for is a better story than one where you did.

> **Internal document.** This is shared inside the Emergency Services Discord. It is not linked from the public rulebook, and it is not for civilians.

## Where to start

* Read [General Conduct](conduct.md) and [Use of Force](use-of-force.md) before your first patrol.
* [Pursuits & PIT](pursuits.md) is the one you will be held to most precisely. Know it properly.
* [Chain of Command](chain-of-command.md) — use it at all times, and do not skip it.

*Protect and Serve.*
